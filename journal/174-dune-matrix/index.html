Closing note

Looking back

The day we drove all the way to Antwerp just to go to the cinema. Cinemas in the Netherlands were closed due to the COVID-19 pandemic, but The Matrix Resurrections and Dune had both recently been released. So we drove to Belgium to watch them back-to-back. Dune was by far the better movie.
